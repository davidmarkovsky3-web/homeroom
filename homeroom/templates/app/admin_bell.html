How this fits together

Slots are the named things a course can occupy — P1, Lunch, {{ school.support_label }}. A day layout says which slots run on a given day and at what times. Monday and Tuesday can share one layout while Wednesday runs a shorter one; a course in P1 simply doesn't meet on a day whose layout has no P1.
