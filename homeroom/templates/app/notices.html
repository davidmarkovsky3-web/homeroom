What shows up here, and what counts on the bell

Announcements and messages sent to you directly can be dismissed, and those are the only things the bell counts — pressing “Mark all read” always clears it to zero.

Study reminders and missing-work notes are worked out live from your record rather than stored, so there's nothing to dismiss: they disappear by themselves once the test has passed or the work is handed in. They're listed here but never make the bell red.
