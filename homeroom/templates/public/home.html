One system, three very different jobs

Homeroom gives each role exactly the screens they need — and nothing they don't.

🎓

Students

A home dashboard, a live "where am I supposed to be" schedule, the school calendar, their own attendance record, and course selection with ranked 1st and 2nd picks.

📋

Teachers

Period-by-period rosters, one-tap attendance from a phone during passing time, course-scoped calendar events, and a running view of who has been missing class.

🏛️

Administrators

Accounts, sections, bell schedules, the A/B rotation calendar, school-wide attendance rates, course request approvals, and the incoming demo and purchase pipeline.

What's inside

Six core modules, all sharing the same roster and calendar.

Home
