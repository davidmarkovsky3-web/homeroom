Questions we get asked

How is the student count determined?

By your official enrollment on the first day of the contract term. If you grow mid-year, we true up at renewal rather than invoicing again.

Do you charge for teacher or staff accounts?

No. Teacher and administrator accounts are unlimited on every plan, including Core.

Can we pay by purchase order?

Yes — that's the default on the quote request form. We also accept ACH and card for smaller schools.

What does implementation involve?

A bell schedule, a course catalog and a roster export. Most single schools are live in two weeks; districts on the District plan get a dedicated implementation manager and a phased rollout.
